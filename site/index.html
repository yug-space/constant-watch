A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what
you’re keeping.

Is this a screen recorder?+

It periodically captures text from your foreground app using Accessibility and OCR. It doesn’t save screenshots or video. The default interval is 10 seconds, so brief changes between samples can be missed.

Do I need an account or subscription?+

No. This version has no authentication, sign-up, or subscription flow. It runs on your computer with Ollama and the local model. The desktop app stores your journal locally.

What about passwords and private information?+

Password managers are excluded by default, detected secure fields are skipped, and common secret patterns are redacted. Those protections are best-effort. Exclude sensitive apps or pause capture for anything you don’t want stored.

Can I trust every summary?+

Small models can make mistakes. Every journal entry retains its original Accessibility and OCR text, so you can check what was actually visible. Screen text is reference material, never instructions for your assistant.

What can an assistant access through MCP?+

Once you connect it, an assistant can search and read the captured journal, including source text. The MCP server is read-only. The assistant’s own handling of that context depends on the service you use.

Where are my notes?+

On Mac, in Application Support under Constant Watch. On Windows, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Constant Watch. Capture settings shows the folder; you can also download a day as Markdown.
