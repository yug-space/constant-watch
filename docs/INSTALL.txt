CONSTANT WATCH 0.2.0 — APPLE SILICON PREVIEW

INSTALL
1. Drag Constant Watch into Applications.
2. Eject this disk image and open Constant Watch from Applications.
3. Follow onboarding to enable Accessibility and Screen Recording for Constant Watch.
4. Setup starts an installed Ollama engine and downloads Qwen 3.5 0.8B if needed.
   If Ollama is missing, use the official installer offered in setup, then return.
5. Start your journal. The model download can finish in the background.

MEETINGS
Open Meetings to download the separate Whisper base speech model (~150 MB).
Meeting detection prompts you; recording never starts automatically. Choose
microphone, computer audio, or both, and let participants know before recording.
Transcription runs locally after you stop. Computer audio includes all system
sound. Audio is deleted after a successful transcript unless you choose to keep it.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later, and Ollama.
The Python runtime, service dependencies, OCR helper, and onboarding music are
inside the app. No Python, Homebrew, uv, Terminal commands, or source checkout
are needed to run it. The Ollama app and model weights are separate downloads.

This is a preview build. Signing and notarization status are provided alongside
the disk image. A signature alone does not mean Apple has notarized the build.

YOUR DATA
Notes and settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Constant Watch.
Closing the window keeps capture running. Quit Constant Watch to stop.
Pause and app exclusions are available in the app. Screenshots are processed
in memory for OCR; saved journal content is text. No account is required.

MCP
Choose Connect with MCP in the app and copy its configuration. Keep the app
installed at the same location after configuring your MCP client.

UPDATES AND REMOVAL
Quit Constant Watch before replacing the app with an update. Your notes remain
in Application Support. Moving the app to Trash does not delete your notes.
This drag-install package does not automatically register login startup.
